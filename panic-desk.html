      How this market works
      • SUI starts at $2.00. Its exchange price is the truth; the lending market only sees the oracle’s price, and trades happen on one SUI/USDC DEX pool with a 0.3% fee.
      • Collateral: SUI counts at up to 75% LTV and is liquidated past 80%. liqSUI is valued at its redemption rate (1.05 SUI) and counts at up to 70% LTV, liquidated past 75%.
      • Liquidation: bots repay half of an unhealthy loan and take collateral worth the repaid amount plus a 5% bonus. They only do it when selling that collateral on the DEX pays; otherwise loans sit unhealthy.
      • The oracle updates on a 0.3% move or every 5 minutes. If its price gets too old, the lending market pauses borrowing, collateral withdrawals, and liquidations, unless it is set to accept old prices.
      • Lenders can only withdraw the cash left in the pool. Bad debt is shared by whoever is still lending at the end.
      • Redeeming liqSUI returns full value, but the SUI arrives after this session, so it’s counted at the final SUI price.
      • Two hours is too short for interest to matter much. This game is about prices, timing, and who gets out first.
