The rules in this lab
  • One market, SUI. The vAMM starts at 100,000 vSUI and $200,000 of virtual USDC (x × y = k), price $2.
  • Initial margin 10% (max 10×). Maintenance margin 6.25%. Fee: 0.1% of each trade to the insurance fund.
  • Funding each hour = position size × (vAMM − oracle) ÷ 24. Longs pay when the vAMM is above the oracle. The insurance fund pays or keeps whatever longs and shorts don’t cancel. Real v1 uses one-hour averages (TWAPs); this lab uses the prices at that moment.
  • Liquidation below 6.25%: if the margin ratio is still above 1.25%, close 25% and charge 1.25% of it (half keeper, half fund). Otherwise close it all: the keeper gets half of 1.25%, the rest of the margin goes to the fund, and any loss beyond the margin is bad debt the fund pays.
  • Repeg: after 3 funding hours in a row with the gap above 10%, move the vAMM to oracle ± 5% with k unchanged. The fund pays or collects the change in the traders’ net position. If it can’t afford it, the code cuts both reserves by 1% instead.
  • Anyone can call payFunding, which runs funding and then the repeg check.
